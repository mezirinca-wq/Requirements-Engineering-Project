# Week 1 — Initial Discovery

## 1. Facts
1. Equipment can be double-booked.
2. People are unsure if equipment is available.
3. Staff spend time checking bookings manually.
4. Equipment may not be returned on time.
5. Students are unsure who to contact about equipment.
6. Bookings are managed through emails, spreadsheets, and informal arrangements.
7. Shared equipment includes laptops, cameras, projectors, and specialist equipment.
8. The equipment is shared by students and staff.
9. 
## 2. Assumptions
1. The college has a limited number of each type of equipment.
2. There may be limits on how long equipment can be booked.
3. Staff and students may have different booking rules.
4. Different equipment may have different requirements or rules.
5. Equipment availability may change regularly.
6. Users may need permission to access some equipment.
7. Some equipment is currently being used or held by someone.

## 3. Unknowns
1. Whether bookings will be manual or automated.
2. How many students and staff will use the system.
3. What happens if equipment is not returned.
4. Who supplies or maintains the equipment.
5. What budget is needed.
6. What access and security measures are required.
7. 
## 4. Stakeholders
1. Students 
2. Suppliers 
3. IT support 
4. Staff 
5. Security team 
6.College management 

## 5. Goals
1. Reduce double-booking and overbooking.
2. Improve visibility of equipment availability.
3. Improve the tracking of equipment.
4. Encourage equipment to be returned on time.
5. Make it clearer who is responsible for equipment.

## 6. Scope
1. Booking shared college equipment.
2. Checking equipment availability.
3. Tracking bookings and returns.
4. Managing equipment information.
5. Supporting students and staff who use the equipment.
6. Recording equipment that is currently available or unavailable.

## Out of scope / Unknown
1. The technology that will be used.
2. Who will maintain the equipment.
3. How equipment will be physically maintained.
4. The budget for the system.
5. Whether booking will be manual or automated.

## 7. Candidate Requirements
Functional Requirements:
1. The system shall allow an authorised user to request equipment for a specified date and time.
2. The system shall show whether equipment is available for a specified date and time.
3. The system shall record equipment bookings and prevent double-booking.
4. The system shall record when equipment is returned.

Non-Functional Requirements:
1. The system shall protect user and booking information from unauthorised access.
2. The system shall be reliable and available when users need to make or check bookings.
